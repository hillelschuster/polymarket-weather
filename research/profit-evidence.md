## Economic interpretation

The observed profit record supports a **barbell weather program**:

### Engine A — high-frequency city extrema

Daily highs/lows offer:

- recurring opportunities every day;
- many cities;
- observation-conditioned certainty collapse;
- source/model latency;
- full-ladder trading;
- fast bankroll turnover.

Individual contract capacity is smaller than major climate markets, but recurrence can make aggregate PnL substantial.

### Engine B — high-capacity climate/index basis

Monthly GISTEMP and hottest-month/year contracts offer:

- much larger position sizes;
- slower information half-life;
- scheduled publications;
- cross-dataset basis modeling;
- related monthly/annual markets that can share one latent climate model.

The leaderboard's largest dollar wins disproportionately come from this family.

The project should therefore optimize both **cashflow frequency** and **capital capacity**, then allocate capital by marginal expected net dollars rather than pick one family philosophically.

## Research allocation implication

A useful first-dollar research allocation is:

1. daily-temperature/resolver collector because it produces frequent forward evidence quickly;
2. climate-index historical basis model in parallel because it can support much larger eventual size;

This ordering is about information density and expected dollars, not a prerequisite chain.
